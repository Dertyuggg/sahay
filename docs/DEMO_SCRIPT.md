# SAHAY-24 Demo Script

**Total Time:** 3 Minutes
**Goal:** Prove that SAHAY-24 solves real-world accessibility banking problems in an elegant, seamless way.

## 0:00 - 0:30 | The Hook (The Problem)
*Presenter on stage with slides*
"Imagine trying to check your bank balance, but the screen is a blur of tiny text, or your hands shake too much to hit a microscopic 'Transfer' button. For millions of blind, elderly, and motor-impaired individuals, modern banking apps aren't just frustrating—they are unusable. Today, we present SAHAY-24. Not an 'accessibility mode' bolted onto an existing app, but a banking experience built *from the ground up* for true inclusion."

## 0:30 - 1:15 | Persona 1: The Elderly / Low Literacy User
*Switch to screen share (App Home Screen - High Contrast / Large Text Mode)*
"Meet Arthur. He gets overwhelmed by complex menus. With SAHAY-24, Arthur is greeted by a high-contrast, clutter-free dashboard. Notice the typography—large, highly legible. There's one primary action on the screen. He taps the massive 'Pay Bills' icon, paired with clear text. No jargon. Just simple, guided steps."

## 1:15 - 2:00 | Persona 2: The Motor-Impaired User
*Show keyboard navigation across the app*
"Now, let's look at Sarah, who has a motor impairment and relies on a keyboard. Watch as I navigate the entire transfer process without a mouse."
*(Demonstrate hitting TAB)*
"Notice the prominent focus rings. Every hit area is at least 44x44 pixels. There are no complex swipe gestures required. She inputs the amount, hits enter on the oversized 'Confirm' button, and the transaction is secure and complete."

## 2:00 - 2:45 | Persona 3: The Visually Impaired User
*Turn on screen reader output / Voice feature*
"Finally, for our blind and visually impaired users, semantic HTML and ARIA roles are woven into the DNA of SAHAY-24. But we went further. By integrating our VoiceNavigator, users can hear their balance and confirm transactions through guided audio prompts, completely bypassing visual bottlenecks."
*(Trigger a brief voice prompt or screen reader demo)*

## 2:45 - 3:00 | The Close
"SAHAY-24 isn't just a hackathon project; it's a blueprint for the future of financial inclusion. Because banking is a fundamental right, and your app should never be the barrier. Thank you."
